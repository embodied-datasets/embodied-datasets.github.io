nuPlan

Also known as nuPlan v1.1

Motional's large-scale planning dataset with more than a thousand hours of auto-labelled logs, HD maps and a closed-loop simulator, from four cities.

Available

Overview

nuPlan trades nuScenes' human annotations for scale. It ships 15,910 logs from Boston, Pittsburgh, Las Vegas and Singapore with auto-labelled agent tracks, inferred traffic-light states and city-wide HD maps, plus a simulator that replays or reacts to the logged traffic. It was built for closed-loop planning research: the benchmark scores how a planner drives when its own actions change the scene, not how well it imitates a recorded trajectory.

Only a 135 GB core of databases and maps is needed for planning work. Raw cameras and LiDAR exist for a roughly 120-hour subset and add hundreds of gigabytes per split.

History and availability

nuPlan is one of the few large datasets served from an anonymously readable cloud bucket, so no account stands between you and the data. The licence is still non-commercial. The 2023 challenge has concluded, but the data and the devkit remain online and the ecosystem (NAVSIM, py123d, many planners) keeps the format alive. The devkit itself is no longer actively maintained and pins old dependencies, which is the most likely future failure mode.

Access

Download the log databases, the map package and optionally the camera and LiDAR shards from the public S3 bucket. py123d's downloader fetches and extracts them into the expected layout; the nuplan-mini-stream config is an end-to-end smoke test, and nuplan-tar converts the sensor archives without extracting their roughly 150 million files.

Quirks and errata

  • Boxes come from an auto-labelling pipeline and can be noisy; traffic-light states are inferred from traffic flow.
  • Sensors and ego state show small synchronisation offsets in the sensor subset.
  • The HD maps are 2D.

Dataset docs

Read this dataset through py123d's unified Arrow format and API. nuplan-tar reads camera images and LiDAR sweeps straight from the downloaded sensor archives after indexing them once.

pip install "py123d[nuplan] plus nuplan-devkit v1.2"
py123d-conversion dataset=nuplan
