nuReasoning

A reasoning-centric long-tail driving dataset of 20-second clips with eight cameras, LiDAR, HD maps and human-verified spatial, decision and counterfactual reasoning annotations.

byUCLA Mobility Lab,Motional· released May 2026· last update Sep 2026
Registration required
Free, but you must create an account and/or accept terms before downloading (login walls, gated Hugging Face repositories with automatic approval). Last verified 14 Oct 2026 by an agent. Non-commercial use under the nuScenes Terms of Use; commercial use needs a separate licence from Motional.
Evidence ↗Registration ↗

Overview

nuReasoning extends the nuScenes recipe towards language. Every clip is a self-contained 20-second long-tail scenario with eight cameras, ego state, a per-clip HD map and traffic-signal context, and about one frame per second carries human-verified reasoning annotations in three categories: spatial reasoning (what is where), driving decisions (what to do and why) and counterfactuals (what would happen if). Two benchmarks build on this: a reasoning VQA task and a planning task.

History and availability

The authors release the data in stages. A mini release accompanied the paper in spring 2026; the full staged release with 11,890 clips followed on the Hugging Face Hub in August 2026. The remaining clips are announced for after the nuReasoning Challenge 2026. Access is gated: accept the terms with a Hugging Face account and approval is automatic. The licence is the non-commercial nuScenes terms of use.

Access

Request access on the Hugging Face dataset page, then provide a token. py123d's downloader selects clips by split, part or name and extracts the per-clip archives; the stream configs download, convert and delete in one go. Plan for about 27 TB if you want everything.

Quirks and errata

  • The test split has no ground truth: cameras, ego states and route only, plus the challenge questions on the key frame.
  • Most clips list their key frame twice; py123d stores it once, so converted frame indices can be one lower than the upstream frame_index.
  • LiDAR is present only in some clips and never in the test split.
