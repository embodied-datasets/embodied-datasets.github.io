TruckDrive

Torc Robotics' long-range highway dataset for heavy trucks, with multi-camera and multi-LiDAR (FMCW and Ouster) rigs, 3D boxes and lane maps.

byTorc Robotics· released Mar 2026· last update Jul 2026

Evidence ↗Registration ↗

Overview

TruckDrive targets a gap in public data: highway driving from the perspective of a heavy truck, where reliable perception hundreds of metres ahead is needed for anticipatory planning and safe braking. The rig combines multiple cameras with a long-range FMCW LiDAR and three Ouster LiDARs, and the labels include 3D boxes and lane-level map annotations per scene.

History and availability

The paper appeared in March 2026 and the data followed on the Hugging Face Hub in June under a custom non-commercial licence with automatic gating.

Access

Request access on the Hugging Face page, export a token and download selected scenes with py123d-download dataset=truckdrive. Test scenes carry no annotations and are skipped by py123d.

Quirks and errata

  • Sensor frequencies and annotation completeness vary by scene.
  • This record is a stub: scale and sensor details still need to be filled from the paper.
