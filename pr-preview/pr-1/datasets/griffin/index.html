Griffin

A CARLA-rendered aerial-ground cooperative perception dataset pairing a ground vehicle with a drone, with 3D boxes for cooperative detection and tracking.

byTsinghua University, School of Vehicle and Mobility· released Mar 2025· last update Sep 2025
Available
Publicly downloadable without an account (direct link, open cloud bucket, torrent, open Hugging Face repository). Last verified 14 Oct 2026 by an agent.
Evidence ↗1 mirror listed ↓

Overview

Griffin pairs a ground vehicle with a drone flying above it and asks how much a bird's-eye partner helps 3D detection and tracking. It is rendered in CARLA with AirSim handling the drone, so every object comes with exact boxes and an occlusion measure. The 255 scenes are organised in four subsets by drone altitude, from a fixed low altitude up to randomised heights.

History and availability

Released in 2025 under the MIT licence on the Hugging Face Hub, with a Baidu Netdisk copy for users in China. No account or gating is involved, and the permissive licence allows redistribution.

Access

Download the subset folders from Hugging Face and extract the griffin-release trees. py123d converts the vehicle side by default and the drone side with the griffin_drone config; the official train and val partitions are embedded in py123d.

Quirks and errata

  • Non-traffic CARLA categories such as props are outside the perception taxonomy and are skipped.
  • The vehicle and drone logs of a scene share a global frame but are not yet modelled as one cooperative scene.

Read this dataset through py123d's unified Arrow format and API. Vehicle and drone are converted as separate logs sharing one global frame; cooperative views are not a first-class type yet.

pip install "py123d[griffin]"
py123d-conversion dataset=griffin
