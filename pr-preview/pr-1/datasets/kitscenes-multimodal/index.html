KITScenes Multimodal

Also known as KITScenes

KIT's European urban dataset with nine global-shutter cameras, seven long-range LiDARs, three 4D radars and the most complete Lanelet2 HD maps of any public driving dataset.

Overview

KITScenes Multimodal is built for Level 4 robotaxi requirements in European cities: nine high-resolution global-shutter cameras with full surround coverage, seven dense long-range LiDARs, three 4D imaging radars and Lanelet2 HD maps covering 62 km² with full topology between lanes, signs and traffic lights. The maps have been validated in closed-loop drives with Autoware. The early release holds more than 1,000 scenes of 20 seconds from Karlsruhe, Frankfurt and Sindelfingen and ships without object boxes; map perception, novel view synthesis and end-to-end driving are the intended benchmarks.

History and availability

Published as an early release on the Hugging Face Hub in May 2026 with the paper following in June. Access is gated with automatic approval after accepting the CC BY-NC terms. Expect the dataset to grow and change; py123d marks its support as experimental for the same reason.

Access

Accept the terms on the Hugging Face page and log in. Every scene is one archive of a few gigabytes; py123d's downloader extracts scenes into the expected layout and the stream config converts them in one go.

Quirks and errata

  • The ego vehicle's generation and dimensions are not published; py123d assumes a long-wheelbase BMW 7 Series.
  • Corner LiDARs see parts of the ego vehicle; those points are not removed.
  • Lanelet2 has no intersection primitive, so intersections are derived from crossing lanes.

Known issues
  • No 3D bounding boxes in the early release.
  • LiDAR points and radar velocities are not ego-motion compensated.
  • Timestamps are relative to the scene start; no absolute time is published.

py123d experimental

Dataset docs

Read this dataset through py123d's unified Arrow format and API. Converts ego trajectory, all cameras, LiDARs and radars and the per-scene Lanelet2 maps without the lanelet2 library. Docs page not yet deployed to kesai.eu.

pip install "py123d[hf]"
py123d-conversion dataset=kitscenes
Configs:kitsceneskitscenes-stream
