nuScenes

Also known as nuScenes v1.0

Motional's 1,000-scene multimodal benchmark from Boston and Singapore with six cameras, a LiDAR, five radars and HD maps.

Overview

nuScenes was the first large-scale driving dataset to ship the full sensor suite of a production-style autonomous vehicle: six cameras, a LiDAR and five radars, all calibrated and synchronised, together with HD maps and 1,400,000 human-annotated 3D boxes across 1,000 scenes of 20 seconds each. It set the template that nuPlan, Argoverse 2 and many later datasets followed: short self-contained scenes, keyframe annotations and a Python devkit with an official evaluation server.

The scenes were recorded in Boston and Singapore, which gives left- and right-hand traffic, two climates and dense urban driving at day and night, in sun and in rain.

History and availability

The dataset has been continuously available since its full release in 2019, always behind a free account. Later expansions added point-wise LiDAR segmentation and panoptic labels without changing the terms. The non-commercial licence is the main constraint for industry users; Motional offers commercial licences separately.

Access

Create an account on the nuScenes website and accept the terms of use. The download page lists the archives (trainval metadata and ten blob archives, test, mini, map expansion and CAN bus). The whole dataset is about 700 GB; the mini split is small enough for a laptop.

With py123d installed with the nuscenes extra, the downloader handles the login flow and the archives for you and the conversion produces the unified Arrow format. The nuscenes-interpolated configs lift the 2 Hz annotations to 10 Hz for training that needs denser supervision.

Quirks and errata

  • Annotations exist only at the 2 Hz keyframes; the sweeps in between carry ego poses but no boxes.
  • The HD maps are 2D. Height information has to come from the LiDAR.
  • Radar point clouds are sparse and were ignored by most early work, which is worth knowing when comparing published baselines.

Dataset docs

Read this dataset through py123d's unified Arrow format and API. The interpolated configs upsample the 2 Hz keyframe annotations to 10 Hz using the intermediate LiDAR sweeps and CAN bus data.

pip install "py123d[nuscenes]"
py123d-conversion dataset=nuscenes
