Argoverse 2 Sensor

Also known as AV2 Sensor, AV2-Sensor

Argo AI's 1,000-log perception dataset from six US cities with seven ring cameras, a stereo pair, two LiDARs, 3D HD maps and 3D cuboids in 30 categories.

byArgo AI· released Jun 2021
Available
Publicly downloadable without an account (direct link, open cloud bucket, torrent, open Hugging Face repository). Last verified 14 Oct 2026 by an agent. Non-commercial use under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 and the Argoverse terms of use.
Evidence ↗

Overview

The Argoverse 2 Sensor dataset is the perception half of Argo AI's second data release. Each of its 1,000 logs covers 15 seconds in one of six US cities with seven ring cameras, a forward stereo pair, two stacked LiDARs and a 3D HD map with lane geometry and ground height. Objects are annotated as 3D cuboids in a long-tailed taxonomy that goes well beyond the usual car-pedestrian-cyclist split, which was one of the dataset's stated goals.

History and availability

Argoverse 2 is the clearest example in this wiki of a dataset outliving its creator. Argo AI closed in October 2022, yet the website is maintained and the data sits on a public, anonymously readable S3 bucket. There is no login and no application: a plain S3 client is enough, which is why py123d uses it as its demo dataset. The non-commercial licence still applies.

Access

Copy logs from the public bucket with any S3 client or use py123d's downloader, which can fetch a handful of validation logs for a quick look. The complete dataset is about 250 GB.

Quirks and errata

  • The ego-vehicle parameters in py123d are partially estimated.
  • Map polygons are derived from polylines and can carry conversion artefacts.

Dataset docs

Read this dataset through py123d's unified Arrow format and API. The stream config pulls selected logs from S3 and needs no cloud account; it is py123d's default demo dataset.
