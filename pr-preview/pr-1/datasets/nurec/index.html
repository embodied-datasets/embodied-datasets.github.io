NuRec (PhysicalAI-AV NuRec)

Also known as PhysicalAI-Autonomous-Vehicles-NuRec

Neural-reconstruction scenes of NVIDIA driving clips – 3D Gaussian reconstructions with ego poses, auto-labelled tracks and HD maps for closed-loop simulation.

Overview

NuRec is a dataset of simulators rather than recordings. Each of its 1,607 scenes is a .usdz archive covering about 20 seconds of a real Hyperion drive, with the ego trajectory, auto-labelled cuboid tracks, an HD map and a 3D Gaussian reconstruction that renders camera views from any pose. No recorded frames are shipped; cameras are rendered, which is what makes closed-loop end-to-end evaluation in AlpaSim possible. py123d converts the log and the map and leaves the reconstruction assets alone, or adds the real sensors of the underlying Physical AI AV clip with the nurec-pai config.

History and availability

The Hugging Face repository dates from mid-2025; the data used today comes from the 26.01 and 26.04 releases, the latter with complete clipgt map layers for every scene. Access is gated with automatic approval under the NVIDIA AV dataset licence.

Access

Accept the licence, export a token and fetch scenes with py123d-download dataset=nurec-curated (the AlpaSim train and val scenes) or per release. Scenes are about two gigabytes each.

Quirks and errata

  • Traffic-light states are not recorded; stop zones are typed by the light or sign that controls them.
  • Some scenes of release 26.01 only carry an OpenDRIVE map, which is sparser and mislabels km/h limits as mph.
  • Frames are placed on an exact 10 Hz grid by interpolation because the source timestamps are only nominally uniform.

Known issues
  • No per-timestep traffic-light states; only the geometry of lights and signs.
  • Rig timestamps drift by milliseconds and track timestamps run on a separate clock.

Dataset docs

Read this dataset through py123d's unified Arrow format and API. nurec-pai adds the cameras and LiDAR of the underlying Physical AI AV clip to each scene. Docs page not yet deployed to kesai.eu.
