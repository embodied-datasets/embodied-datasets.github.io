NVIDIA Physical AI Autonomous Vehicles

Also known as PAI-AV, PhysicalAI-Autonomous-Vehicles, Physical AI AV

NVIDIA's gated release of more than 300,000 Hyperion 8 clips with seven fisheye cameras at 30 fps, a 360° LiDAR, high-rate egomotion and auto-labelled 3D boxes.

Overview

PAI-AV is by far the largest dataset in this wiki: 307,332 clips of about 20 seconds, 1,707 hours and 69,265.7 km recorded on NVIDIA's Hyperion 8 platform. Each clip carries seven f-theta fisheye cameras at 30 fps, a 360° LiDAR at 10 Hz with per-point timestamps, high-rate egomotion from two sources and auto-labelled 3D boxes. There are no HD maps and no human labels; the dataset is meant for pretraining and world-model work at scale.

History and availability

Published on the Hugging Face Hub in 2025 behind NVIDIA's dataset licence with automatic gating, and still updated in 2026. Because the data is packed in archives of about a hundred clips per sensor, fetching even a few clips means downloading large chunks; py123d therefore reads the chunk archives directly and NuRec's downloader pulls single clips out of them with HTTP range requests.

Access

Accept the licence on the Hugging Face page and export a token. Set the data root and run the physical-ai-av conversion; archives do not need to be extracted. Clips that lack LiDAR or egomotion can be skipped with a flag.

Quirks and errata

  • Boxes are auto-generated and noisier than human labels.
  • Some clips are incomplete and fail conversion unless skipped.
  • The f-theta camera model has five polynomial coefficients; py123d pads them to six for NCore compatibility.
